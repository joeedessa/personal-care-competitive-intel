Category spread

How many times the cheapest option the dearest one costs, on the same basis. A wide spread means the category tolerates a premium; a narrow one means you are competing on something other than price.

UAE price gap

Observed AED shelf price against the FX-parity expectation (US price × 3.6725 × tier uplift). Positive means the UAE consumer pays more than parity — pricing power, or a grey-market opening. Fill px.aed in the data files as you verify real shelf prices.
