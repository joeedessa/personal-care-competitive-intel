Category spread — how much premium each category tolerates

What it measures. Within one category, on a like-for-like basis, how many times the cheapest option the dearest one costs. Body lotion at 41× means the most expensive body lotion tracked costs 41 times the cheapest per 100 ml.

How to read it. A wide spread means customers in that category already accept an enormous premium for brand — the ceiling is high and there is room to price up. A narrow spread means price is compressed and everyone is clustered: you would be competing on distribution, design or story rather than on where you sit on the ladder.

The caveat. Spread is driven by its endpoints, so a single mass benchmark at the bottom stretches it. The named brands at each end tell you whether the number is real or an artefact — filter to Luxury + Premium to see the spread among genuine competitors.
